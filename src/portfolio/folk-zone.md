- Federated social services including Mastodon, WriteFreely, Pixelfed, and Lemmy

- Self-hosted on consumer hardware with Caddy reverse proxy and Docker Compose
- Custom Eleventy homepage with accessible brand system and design tokens
- Invite-only community model with open registration for the git forge
